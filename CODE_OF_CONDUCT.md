# Code of conduct

PulseCheck adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and respectful, assume good intent, give and accept constructive feedback gracefully, and focus on what is best for the people who use PulseCheck. Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and publishing other people's private information are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

To report unacceptable behavior, open a private [security advisory](https://github.com/Viniciusadm/pulsecheck/security/advisories/new) addressed to the maintainers or contact them through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits and other contributions that break this code, and may ban contributors temporarily or permanently.
